We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of every devotee who visits this portal. Please read this policy carefully to understand how we handle your information.

1. Who We Are

Organisation Name: Bageshwar Bala Ji Sundarkand Mandal, Nagla Dallu

Nature: Non-profit, voluntary religious community organisation dedicated to the propagation of Sundarkand Path and devotion to Shri Bageshwar Bala Ji and Shri Hanuman Ji.

Location: Nagla Dallu, Uttar Pradesh, India

Website: balajisundarkand.netlify.app

Contact Email: balajisundarkandnagladallu@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you access or use our website ("the Website"). By continuing to use this Website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following circumstances:

2.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Donation Submissions: When you choose to make a donation through this Website, you may voluntarily provide your name, UPI transaction ID, donation amount, donation mode (e.g., UPI, Cash), and an optional personal note or message. Anonymous donations are also accepted and we do not compel the disclosure of identity.
  • Contact & Enquiries: If you contact us via the email address provided, we may receive your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • WhatsApp Group: If you join our WhatsApp community group via the link on this Website, your phone number and WhatsApp display name will be visible to group members as governed by WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We may collect non-personally identifiable information such as the pages you visit, the duration of your visit, and the device/browser type. This is collected through basic server logs maintained by Netlify (our hosting provider).
  • IP Addresses: Your IP address may be logged by Netlify's infrastructure for security, anti-abuse, and DDoS-protection purposes. We do not use IP addresses to identify you personally.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect credit card, debit card, or banking credentials. Donations are processed via UPI (Unified Payments Interface) directly between you and the payment app — we only record the transaction ID you voluntarily provide to us after payment.
  • We do not track your behaviour across other websites.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party ad networks.
  • We do not collect biometric data, Aadhaar numbers, or any government ID.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following religious and administrative purposes:

  • Recording and Acknowledging Donations: To verify, record, and publicly acknowledge (on this Website) donations made to the Mandal, so that the community can see the supporters of this divine cause. Anonymous donation records are displayed without any identifying information.
  • Administrative Communication: To respond to your queries or contact you regarding donation verification or event information, if you have reached out to us first.
  • Website Operations: To maintain, operate, and improve the functionality and security of the Website.
  • Event and Programme Information: To display information about upcoming Sundarkand Path events, announcements, and religious programmes relevant to devotees.
  • Spiritual Content Delivery: To display Sundarkand Path text, live streaming links, past recordings, and gallery images for the benefit of all devotees.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations, including responding to lawful requests from competent authorities.

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose whatsoever. Our mission is entirely spiritual and community-based.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (PDPB / IT Act)

This Website is operated from India and is governed by Indian law. The applicable legal frameworks include:

  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, which require us to maintain reasonable security practices when handling sensitive personal data such as financial information.
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) — insofar as applicable to our scale and nature of operations as a small religious community organisation.

Our legal basis for processing personal data is:

  • Consent: You voluntarily provide your name and transaction ID when submitting a donation. This is an explicit act of consent.
  • Legitimate Interests: Operating a transparent, accountable religious organisation and maintaining the integrity of our donation records constitutes a legitimate interest.
  • Legal Obligation: We may process data to comply with applicable laws, court orders, or government directions.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We treat the information you provide with the utmost respect and confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, trade, or commercially exploit your personal information.

Your information may only be shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within the Mandal: Donation records are accessible to the authorised administrator of the Mandal for verification and record-keeping purposes only.
  • Public Display of Donations: If you submit a non-anonymous donation, your name and the fact that you are a supporter may be displayed publicly on the Homepage of this Website as an acknowledgement of your generosity. No financial amounts are publicly displayed. You may choose to donate anonymously to prevent this.
  • Service Providers: We use the following technical service providers who may process data on our behalf:
    • Google Firebase (Firestore & Authentication): Donation records, event data, member data, gallery captions, and settings are stored in Google Firebase Firestore. Firebase is a Google product governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
    • Cloudinary: Gallery images and member photos are hosted on Cloudinary. Images uploaded by the administrator are stored on Cloudinary's servers.
    • Netlify: This Website is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may collect server logs as part of its service.
    • Google YouTube: Live streams and past recordings are embedded from YouTube. Viewing embedded videos is subject to Google/YouTube's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal Requirement: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or lawful governmental authority.

  • Google Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Cloudinary: cloudinary.com/privacy
  • Netlify: netlify.com/privacy
  • Google / YouTube: policies.google.com/privacy

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy:

  • Donation Records: Donation records (including your name and transaction ID, where provided) are retained indefinitely for the purpose of maintaining accurate spiritual community records, as is customary for religious organisations. However, upon written request, we will delete your specific donation record if it is not required for legal or audit purposes.
  • Gallery Images: Images uploaded to the gallery are retained until an administrator removes them from the system.
  • Server Logs: Netlify's server logs are retained for a limited period as per Netlify's data retention policy, over which we have no direct control.
  • Administrative Data: Admin login credentials are managed directly within Firebase Authentication and are controlled by the Mandal administrator.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection law (IT Rules 2011, DPDPA 2023) and as a matter of respect for our devotees, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to know what personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain certain records.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. This will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to file a grievance with us regarding the processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via email at balajisundarkandnagladallu@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within a reasonable period, typically within 30 days.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

This Website uses minimal technical mechanisms for its operation:

  • Firebase SDK Cookies: The Firebase JavaScript SDK may set cookies or use local storage to manage authentication sessions for the administrator login panel. These are essential technical cookies required for site security.
  • No Analytics Cookies: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other third-party analytics or advertising cookies on this Website.
  • No Tracking Pixels: We do not embed any invisible tracking pixels or beacons from advertising networks.
  • Browser Local Storage: Some features of this Website may use your browser's local storage for temporary performance improvements (e.g., caching settings). No personal data is stored in local storage.

Since we use only essential technical cookies, a cookie consent banner is not mandatorily required under current Indian law. However, you can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

10. Children's Privacy

This Website is a religious and cultural resource open to all ages. However, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18 without parental consent.

Our donation form requests voluntary submission of name and transaction details. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise their children's use of the Website. Any donation made by a minor should be made with the knowledge and permission of a parent or legal guardian.

If you believe that a child under the age of 18 has provided personal information to us without parental consent, please contact us immediately at balajisundarkandnagladallu@gmail.com and we will take prompt steps to delete such information.

11. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and have implemented the following reasonable security measures:

  • Firebase Security Rules: Our Firestore database is protected by granular security rules that restrict write access to authorised administrators only. Public users can only submit new donation records (not read or modify others' records).
  • Authentication: The admin dashboard is protected by Firebase Authentication (email and password). Only a single authorised administrator email can access the admin panel.
  • HTTPS: This Website is served exclusively over HTTPS (enforced by Netlify), ensuring that all data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • No Payment Data Storage: We do not store any payment credentials, UPI PINs, or financial account details. We only record the transaction ID you voluntarily provide after completing your payment independently.
  • Cloudinary Upload Security: Gallery images are uploaded using an unsigned upload preset. The Cloudinary account itself is protected by strong credentials accessible only to the administrator.

However, please note that no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the Website after any changes to this Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.

For significant changes that materially affect how we handle your personal data, we will endeavour to provide a notice on the Homepage or via the scrolling announcement bar on the Website.
